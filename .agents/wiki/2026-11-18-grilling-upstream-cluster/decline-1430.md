# Declined: impact, scope, and confidence markers per question

- 2026-11-18: Declined mattpocock/skills#1430 for this fork. The issue asks for a compact marker on every question: impact (how much it shapes the outcome and whether it can be undone), scope (core to the plan or could wait), and confidence in the recommendation.
- 2026-11-18: Reasons, in order:
  - The fork mainline's round format already forces each question to carry its diverging options and the reason the recommended option beats the others (`CompileNix/skills@be92972`). The per-decision weight is visible in the written round, and the question-tool step presents each decision separately with its options, which breaks the batch rubber-stamping the issue's first anecdote describes.
  - A standing three-part marker on every question in every round is format weight the skill's deliberately minimal round format resists. The issue cites its own precedent: mattpocock/skills#125, the heavier earlier attempt, was closed as too heavy, and the issue's author concedes the minimal form is the confidence half alone.
  - The impact and scope halves are partly recovered by the options format: a decision with real alternatives gets them spelled out with a recommendation, so the user sees the shape of the choice before answering.
- 2026-11-18: The confidence half (how sure the recommendation is, stated with the recommendation) is filed as a follow-up candidate, not pursued in this run. Revisit if a session still rubber-stamps a high-impact, hard-to-undo decision.
